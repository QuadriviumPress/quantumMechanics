# Sources and attribution

## Primary text

*Quantum Mechanics: A Spins-First, Experimental Approach* is an original
QuadriviumPress textbook distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Figures and external resources

New prose, figures, exercises, simulations, and datasets must identify any
third-party sources and their licenses here or next to the material they
support. Do not copy material from sources that do not permit redistribution
and adaptation.

## Embedded simulations

Interactive figures embed simulations hosted by their authors, in an iframe,
with a caption link back to the running simulation; no simulation code is
copied into this repository. See [`plugins/README.md`](plugins/README.md) for
how the `{phet}`, `{openlyceum}`, and `{phet-legacy}` directives work. Two
suppliers are used, both of which require attribution and receive it through
that link:

- **PhET Interactive Simulations** (University of Colorado Boulder), CC-BY 4.0.
  The `{phet}` directive addresses PhET's HTML5 catalogue; `{phet-legacy}`
  addresses its pre-HTML5 Java simulations, which PhET now runs in the browser
  through CheerpJ and which remain the only interactive treatment of bound
  states and tunneling.
- **OpenLyceum**, AGPL-3.0, via the `{openlyceum}` directive.
